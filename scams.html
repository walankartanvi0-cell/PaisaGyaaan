🚨 One thing almost every scam has in common

The scammer wants you to make a decision quickly: pay, click, share, install, transfer or reveal.

When something involves your money or sensitive information, slowing down is a security habit — not a weakness.

01 · KNOW THE PATTERNS

Common financial scams

Scammers change their stories constantly, but many of their techniques follow familiar patterns.

🏦
BANKING

Fake KYC scam

You receive a message claiming that your bank account or wallet will be blocked unless you update KYC.

  • Urgent deadline
  • Suspicious link
  • Request for personal details
  • Threat of account suspension
📲
UPI

Fake UPI payment

Someone claims they are sending you money but asks you to enter your UPI PIN or approve a payment.

  • “Enter PIN to receive money”
  • Unknown collect request
  • Pressure to act quickly
📈
INVESTMENT

Fake investment adviser

A person promises unusually high or guaranteed returns and asks you to transfer money to a personal account.

  • Guaranteed returns
  • Fake screenshots
  • Limited-time opportunity
  • Pressure to invest more
💼
JOB

Fake job scam

You are offered a job but asked to pay registration, training, verification or security fees.

  • Unsolicited job offer
  • Upfront payment
  • Unrealistic salary
  • Pressure to transfer money
📦
DELIVERY

Parcel / customs scam

A caller claims that a parcel linked to you contains illegal items and demands money to “resolve” it.

  • Fear and intimidation
  • Fake officials
  • Immediate payment demand
💳
LOAN

Fake loan app

A person or app offers an instant loan and may demand fees or excessive permissions.

  • Upfront processing fee
  • Unknown application
  • Unnecessary permissions
  • Threatening recovery tactics
🖥️
DEVICE

Remote-access scam

Someone asks you to install an app or share your screen to receive a refund or fix an account problem.

  • Remote-control request
  • Screen-sharing request
  • Banking activity during the call
📱
SOCIAL MEDIA

Fake social-media support

A fake account pretends to represent a bank, company or payment service and asks for account details.

  • Unofficial account
  • Direct-message support
  • Request for OTP or credentials
🧾
MARKET

Fake IPO / trading opportunity

Fraudsters may create fake trading platforms, fake profit screenshots or fake investment groups.

  • Guaranteed profit claims
  • Fake trading dashboard
  • Telegram/WhatsApp pressure
  • Withdrawal problems

03 · THE PATTERN

Learn the red flags

A scam does not always look obviously fake. Multiple small warning signs can be more useful than one clue.

U Urgency

“Do it now.” “Only 10 minutes left.” “Your account will be blocked.”

F Fear

Threats of police action, account closure, legal trouble or financial loss.

G Greed / unrealistic reward

Guaranteed profits, free money, unbelievable discounts or rewards.

S Secret information

Requests for OTPs, PINs, passwords, CVVs or other sensitive credentials.

P Pressure

The person tries to stop you from thinking, asking others or independently verifying.

A Unusual action

Install an app, share your screen, scan a QR code, transfer money or click an unexpected link.

04 · IF YOU SUSPECT FRAUD

Stop. Secure. Report.

If you think you've been scammed, don't spend time blaming yourself. Focus on reducing further damage.

🛑

1. Stop

Stop communicating with the suspected scammer and don't make additional payments.

🔐

2. Secure

Contact your bank/payment provider through official channels and secure affected accounts.

📢

3. Report

Report suspected cyber financial fraud promptly through appropriate official reporting channels.

🇮🇳 Cyber fraud reporting

In India, suspected cyber financial fraud can be reported through the national cybercrime reporting mechanisms.

Cyber fraud helpline
1930

Keep this number in mind if you need to report suspected cyber financial fraud.

Online reporting
🌐

Use the National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal for online complaints.

Financial institution
🏦

Contact your bank or payment provider using independently verified official contact details.
