01

Investment Fundamentals

Start here if the word “investment” still feels complicated.

🎯

Goal

Investing should connect to a purpose. A short-term goal, long-term goal and retirement goal may require different approaches.

⏳

Time Horizon

Time horizon means how long you expect to remain invested before needing the money.

⚖️

Risk

Investment value can fluctuate. Different assets have different types and levels of risk.

💧

Liquidity

Liquidity refers to how easily an investment can be converted into cash without significant delay or loss caused by the transaction itself.

💰

Return

Return represents the gain or loss from an investment. Historical or expected returns are not guarantees of future results.

🧾

Costs

Fees, taxes, spreads and other charges can affect the amount you ultimately receive.

02

Risk & Return

There is no universal investment that is “best” for everyone.

A simple way to think about investment risk

Risk can come from market movements, credit/default, inflation, interest-rate changes, liquidity constraints, concentration and other factors.

Lower Higher
⚠️
Important

“Low risk” does not mean “no risk”. Likewise, “high return” is not guaranteed simply because an investment is considered high-risk.

03

Major Investment Categories

Different assets behave differently. Learn the basic idea before going deeper.

🏦

Bank Deposits

Deposit products can provide relatively predictable interest according to their terms. They are different from market-linked investments.

Examples: Savings accounts, FDs, RDs
📈

Equity

Equity represents ownership in a company. Prices can fluctuate significantly and returns are not guaranteed.

Example: Shares
📜

Debt / Fixed Income

Debt instruments involve lending money to an issuer under specified terms. Credit and interest-rate risks can still exist.

Examples: Bonds, debt securities
- 🪙 + 🥇

Gold

Gold can be held through physical forms or financial products. Each form has different costs, risks and characteristics.

Examples: Physical gold, ETFs, certain government-linked products
🏢

REITs

Real Estate Investment Trusts provide a way to obtain exposure to income-generating real-estate assets through a market-linked structure.

🌱

Pension / Long-Term Products

Certain retirement-oriented products are designed around long-term financial goals and have specific rules regarding contributions and withdrawals.

04

Mutual Funds

A mutual fund pools money from investors and invests according to its stated investment objective and strategy.

🧺

Pooling

Multiple investors contribute money to a common fund, which is managed according to the scheme's objective.

📊

NAV

Net Asset Value represents the per-unit value of a mutual-fund scheme based on its assets and liabilities, subject to the applicable methodology.

🔄

SIP

A Systematic Investment Plan is a method of investing a fixed amount periodically into a mutual-fund scheme.

🧾

Expense Ratio

A mutual fund has operating expenses. The expense ratio represents certain expenses charged to the scheme as specified by applicable regulations.

⚠️

Market Risk

Mutual funds are not automatically risk-free. The level and nature of risk depend on the scheme and its holdings.

🔍

Read the Documents

Understand the scheme objective, portfolio, costs, risk information and applicable terms before investing.

05

Stocks & Equity

Buying a company's share means owning a small part of that company.

🏢

Ownership

Equity shareholders have ownership interest in a company, subject to the rights attached to the particular security.

📈

Price Movement

Share prices can rise or fall because of company-specific, economic, market and other factors.

💰

Dividends

Some companies may distribute part of their profits to shareholders through dividends, subject to applicable decisions and rules.

📚

Research

Investors can study financial statements, business models, valuations, industry conditions and risks.

🧠

Don't Follow Tips Blindly

Social-media tips, Telegram groups and anonymous messages are not substitutes for independent verification.

⚠️

No Guaranteed Returns

Equity investing involves market risk. Past performance cannot guarantee future results.

06

Bonds & Fixed-Income Instruments

Fixed-income investments still require risk awareness.

Concept Meaning What to understand
Principal Original amount invested Understand repayment conditions.
Coupon / Interest Periodic interest according to the instrument's terms Check whether the rate is fixed or variable.
Maturity Date or period when the instrument reaches maturity Consider whether you need liquidity earlier.
Credit Risk Possibility that an issuer fails to meet obligations Higher stated return can sometimes accompany higher credit risk.
Interest-Rate Risk Changes in interest rates can affect market values Particularly relevant for market-traded debt securities.
07

Government-Backed / Long-Term Schemes

Some government-related savings and retirement schemes have specific eligibility, interest, tax and withdrawal rules.

👧

Sukanya Samriddhi

A government-backed savings scheme intended for eligible girl children, subject to its applicable rules and conditions.

🌱

PPF

Public Provident Fund is a long-term savings scheme with defined rules relating to contributions, interest, maturity and withdrawals.

👴

NPS

National Pension System is a retirement-oriented pension framework with its own contribution, investment and withdrawal rules.

📌
Always verify current rules.

Interest rates, tax treatment, eligibility and withdrawal rules can change. Use current official government or regulator information before making a decision.

08

Gold & Gold-Linked Products

“Gold” can mean very different things depending on how it is held.

🪙

Physical Gold

Jewellery, coins and bars involve considerations such as purity, making charges, storage and resale.

📊

Gold ETFs

Exchange-traded products designed to provide exposure to gold prices through a market-linked structure.

📄

Other Gold Products

Other gold-linked products may have different structures, costs, tax treatment and risks. Understand the specific product rather than treating every gold investment as identical.

09

Diversification & Asset Allocation

“Don't put all your eggs in one basket” is useful, but diversification is more than owning many things.

🧺

Diversification

Spreading investments across different assets, sectors or securities can reduce concentration risk.

⚖️

Asset Allocation

Asset allocation concerns how much of a portfolio is exposed to different asset categories.

🔄

Rebalancing

Over time, market movements can change portfolio weights. Rebalancing means adjusting them according to the chosen strategy and rules.

Remember: Diversification ≠ Guaranteed Profit

Diversification can reduce concentration risk, but it cannot eliminate market losses or guarantee a particular return.

11

The Psychology of Investing

Sometimes the biggest investment mistake isn't mathematical. It's behavioural.

🐑 Herd Behaviour

Buying because everyone around you appears to be buying can make you ignore your own analysis and risk tolerance.

😨 Panic Selling

Fear during market declines can lead investors to make rushed decisions without considering their original objective and time horizon.

🤑 FOMO

Fear of missing out can make people chase assets after a sharp rise or act without understanding the underlying product.

🧠 Confirmation Bias

People may look mainly for information that supports what they already believe while ignoring conflicting evidence.

🎰 Recency Bias

Recent performance can feel more important than longer-term evidence, even when the recent period is not representative.

💎 “I Know It Will Rise”

Confidence is not the same thing as certainty. Markets contain uncertainty, and no one can guarantee future prices.

12

Investment Scam Red Flags

Financial literacy also means knowing when to walk away.

🚩
Guaranteed high returns

Be cautious when someone presents high, fixed or guaranteed returns as if risk does not exist.

🚩
“Secret” trading strategy

Claims that only a special group knows a guaranteed market formula deserve scrutiny.

🚩
Pressure to act immediately

Urgency can be used to prevent you from independently verifying an opportunity.

🚩
Asking for OTP / PIN

Never share sensitive authentication information merely because someone claims to be from a financial institution.

🚩
Fake celebrity or expert

Images, names and testimonials can be manipulated. Verify the actual source.

🚩
Unverified apps or links

Avoid installing unknown applications or submitting financial information through suspicious links.

🛑
Stop. Verify. Then decide.

If an investment opportunity cannot be independently verified, don't let urgency force a financial decision.
